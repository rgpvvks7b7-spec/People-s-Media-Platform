## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage and session storage in your browser.

---

## 2. Our commitment: no advertising cookies

IndieFund **does not and will not** use:

- Advertising cookies
- Remarketing or retargeting pixels (Meta Pixel, Google Ads, TikTok Pixel, etc.)
- Third-party ad network tracking
- Sale of personal data to advertisers

**Discovery Ads** on IndieFund are first-party artist promotions billed on fan engagement — not third-party ad network cookies.

If we add third-party **analytics** cookies in future (for example Google Analytics), they will load **only after you consent** through our Iubenda cookie banner. The marketing/advertising category will **never** be enabled.

## 3. How IndieFund uses cookies and similar technologies

### 3.1 Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the Platform to function. We do not need your consent for strictly necessary cookies under most privacy frameworks.

| Name / type | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| **Session cookie** | Keeps you logged in and maintains your authenticated session | Session or up to 2 weeks |
| **CSRF token** (`csrftoken`) | Protects against cross-site request forgery on form submissions and API requests | 1 year |
| **Security settings** | `SameSite=Lax`; `Secure` flag enabled in production | — |

Without these cookies, you cannot log in or perform secure actions on the Platform.

### 3.2 Functional storage (local storage)

We use browser **local storage** (not cookies) for features that improve your experience:

| Key / area | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **Store cart** | Remembers items in your shopping cart between visits |
| **Player preferences** | Volume and playback settings |
| **Discovery mode** | Your discovery view preferences |
| **UI state** | Dismissed notices and interface preferences |
| **Referral source** | Attribution for artist page visits |

You can clear local storage through your browser settings. Doing so may reset preferences and cart contents.

### 3.3 Analytics

**Today:** IndieFund does **not** use third-party analytics cookies. We collect first-party usage data on our servers (page views, plays, purchases) as described in our Privacy Policy.

**Future:** If we add third-party analytics, it will be consent-gated via Iubenda with prior blocking enabled. Advertising cookies will never be added.

### 3.4 Push notifications

If you enable web push notifications, your browser stores a push subscription that our service worker uses to deliver notifications. This requires your explicit browser permission and is optional.

## 4. Cookie consent (Iubenda)

When Iubenda is configured for production, IndieFund displays an Iubenda cookie consent banner:

- **Necessary** and **functional** cookies apply to operate the Platform
- **Analytics** scripts are blocked until you opt in (none loaded today)
- **Marketing/advertising** category is disabled and will never be offered

Manage preferences anytime via the floating preferences button or your browser settings.

When Iubenda is not configured (local development), see the Platform-specific supplement on this page.

## 5. Managing cookies and storage

You can control cookies and local storage through your browser:

- **Block or delete cookies** — may prevent login and core functionality
- **Clear local storage** — resets cart and preferences
- **Disable push notifications** — via browser or IndieFund Profile settings

For browser-specific instructions, see your browser's help documentation.

## 6. Third-party cookies

When you make a payment, you may interact with **Stripe** checkout pages that set their own cookies under Stripe's privacy policy. Embedded social content (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) may set third-party cookies if you interact with those embeds.

IndieFund does not control third-party cookies. Review the relevant provider's policy when using those features.
