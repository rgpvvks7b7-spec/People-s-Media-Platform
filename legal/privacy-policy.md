## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Account and profile information

When you register or update your account, we may collect:

- Username, display name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form)
- Account type (fan, artist, or host)
- Avatar, cover image, and bio
- For fans: favourite genres, city or region, discovery preferences
- For artists: stage name, genre, city, artist story, influences, creative professions, social media URLs, and page settings
- For hosts: venue name, contact email, phone, address, and city

### 3.2 Support, payment, and transaction information

When you subscribe, tip, purchase products, buy tickets, or pay for artist platform plans, we collect transaction details including amounts, dates, and product or subscription identifiers. Payment card details are processed by **Stripe**; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.

We may store Stripe customer IDs, subscription IDs, and payout account identifiers linked to your IndieFund account.

### 3.3 Content you upload or create

If you are an artist or host, we process content you upload or publish, including music files, artwork, posts, stories, live stream metadata, store listings, and venue listing photos. This may include metadata such as genre, AI disclosure level, and subscriber-only settings.

### 3.4 Usage and journey data

We collect first-party analytics about how you use the Platform, including:

- Page views, artist profile visits, and discovery interactions
- Music previews and full plays
- Follows, subscriptions, tips, and purchases
- Comment and like activity
- Email opt-in events
- Promotion campaign impressions and engagement (for artists running Discovery Ads)

We do not use third-party advertising cookies and **do not and will not** use remarketing or ad network tracking (see our [Cookie Policy](/?page=cookies)). If we add third-party analytics cookies in future, they will load only after consent via Iubenda; advertising cookies will never be used.

### 3.5 Communications and notifications

- In-app notifications and optional web push notification subscription data (browser endpoint and encryption keys)
- Email messages we send (gig alerts, purchase receipts, supporter updates, verification emails)
- Beta feedback you submit, which may include page path, summary, details, and browser user agent

### 3.6 Email sharing preferences

Fans may opt in to share their email address with artists they support. We record:

- Global email sharing consent and timestamp
- Per-artist sharing status, source, and revocation timestamps

Artists may export mailing lists only for fans who have opted in.

### 3.7 Technical information

- Session and security cookies (see Cookie Policy)
- IP address and request logs (for security, fraud prevention, and service operation)
- Information stored in browser local storage (for example, cart contents and player preferences)

## 4. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Operate the Platform and deliver features you request
- Process payments, subscriptions, tips, purchases, and artist payouts via Stripe
- Display and stream content according to artist settings (including subscriber-only gating)
- Personalise discovery recommendations and promoted content (respecting your discovery preferences)
- Send transactional emails and, where permitted, supporter or gig notifications
- Detect fraud, enforce our trust and safety systems, and protect the Platform
- Improve the Platform, including during our beta period
- Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

We do not sell your personal information. We **do not and will not** use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers.

We rely on one or more of the following, depending on context:

## 7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to:

- Provide the Platform and honour transactions
- Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements
- Meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements

When you delete your account or request deletion, we will delete or de-identify personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes (for example, transaction records and fraud prevention).

**Draft retention periods (subject to legal review):**

- Account profile data: duration of account plus up to 24 months
- Transaction and payout records: up to 7 years (tax and accounting)
- Server logs: up to 90 days
- Beta feedback: up to 24 months after resolution

## 8. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

- **Access** personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate or outdated information (via Profile settings or by contacting us)
- **Delete** your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Opt out** of sharing your email with artists (Profile → email preferences; revoking global consent revokes all artist shares)
- **Manage notifications** in-app and via browser push settings
- **Adjust discovery preferences** (fewer promoted items, emerging artists preference, genre filters)

To exercise these rights, contact [privacy@indiefund.com](mailto:privacy@indiefund.com). We will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at [www.oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au).
