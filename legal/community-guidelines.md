# Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** 28 June 2026  
**Last updated:** 28 June 2026

> *Draft for review only — not legal advice. Requires review by an Australian technology lawyer before launch.*

---

IndieFund is built for independent human artists and the fans who support them. These Community Guidelines explain what we expect from everyone on the Platform. They supplement our [Terms of Service](/?page=terms).

Violations may result in content removal, upload restrictions, account suspension, or permanent termination.

**Report issues:** [legal@indiefund.com](mailto:legal@indiefund.com)  
**Copyright complaints:** See our [Copyright Policy](/?page=copyright)

---

## 1. Be respectful

Treat artists, fans, hosts, and IndieFund staff with respect.

**Do not:**

- Harass, bully, threaten, or stalk other users
- Use hate speech, slurs, or content that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics
- Dox or share private information about others without consent
- Encourage violence or self-harm

---

## 2. No spam, scams, or deception

**Do not:**

- Send unsolicited bulk messages or repetitive promotional spam
- Run phishing schemes, fake giveaways, or investment scams
- Impersonate artists, brands, IndieFund staff, or other users
- Manipulate discovery rankings, promotion campaigns, or engagement metrics using bots or fake accounts
- Mislead fans about what they are purchasing or subscribing to

---

## 3. Copyright and intellectual property

Upload only content you created or have the rights to use.

**You must:**

- Own or license all music, artwork, samples, beats, images, and video you upload
- Have permission for any third-party material included in your work
- Respect trademarks and artist names

**Copyright complaints:** Follow our [Copyright Policy](/?page=copyright). IndieFund responds to valid infringement notices and maintains a repeat-infringer policy.

---

## 4. AI content policy

IndieFund is for **independent human artists**. AI tools may be used as part of a creative process, but transparency is required.

| Disclosure level | Rule |
|------------------|------|
| **Human-made** | No AI used in the creative work |
| **AI-assisted** | AI used as a tool; human-led creation — **disclosure note required** |
| **AI-collaborative** | Significant human and AI collaboration — **disclosure note required** |
| **AI-generated** | **Not permitted** — fully AI-generated tracks cannot be uploaded |

Disclosure notes must accurately describe how AI was used. Fans see disclosure badges on uploaded tracks.

Instants and posts are not a workaround for uploading full AI-generated songs or bulk content dumps.

---

## 5. Sexual, violent, and illegal content

**Do not upload or share:**

- Illegal content of any kind
- Content that sexualises minors (zero tolerance — we report to authorities)
- Non-consensual intimate imagery
- Excessively graphic violence intended to shock or harass
- Content promoting illegal drugs, weapons, or criminal activity

IndieFund is not primarily an adult-content platform. Sexually explicit content is discouraged and may be removed, especially when not appropriately labelled or when it violates applicable law.

---

## 6. Comments, live chat, and community spaces

### Comments

Artists may set comment access per post:

- Anyone (logged-in viewers who can see the post)
- Followers and subscribers
- Subscribers only
- Account default (artist's profile setting)

Respect the artist's community space. Comments that violate these Guidelines may be removed and accounts may be restricted.

### Live streams and chat

Live sessions may be public, offer a time-limited preview, or be restricted to supporters. Live chat must follow the same standards as comments. Artists may end streams or block disruptive participants.

### Instants

Instants are short-lived posts (up to 280 characters, optional image or short audio). Fans may report Instants they believe violate these Guidelines using the in-app Report button. Reports are reviewed by IndieFund.

---

## 7. Commerce and promotions

**Artists and hosts must:**

- Accurately describe products, tickets, and commissions
- Honour stated fulfilment timelines or communicate delays promptly
- Not sell counterfeit goods or misrepresent merchandise

**Discovery Ads (promoted releases):**

- Campaign budgets are capped ($5–$100 per campaign)
- Billing is based on qualified fan engagement, not raw impressions
- Do not create misleading promotion creative

---

## 8. Venue hosts (IndieFund | Spaces)

Hosts must provide accurate venue information, respond to booking requests in good faith, and comply with local licensing, safety, and accessibility laws.

IndieFund does not take a cut of food and beverage revenue. Do not misrepresent fee arrangements to artists.

---

## 9. How to report

| Issue type | How to report |
|------------|---------------|
| **Instant content** | Use the **Report** button on the Instant |
| **Copyright infringement** | [Copyright Policy](/?page=copyright) — email [copyright@indiefund.com](mailto:copyright@indiefund.com) |
| **Other violations** | Email [legal@indiefund.com](mailto:legal@indiefund.com) with links, screenshots, and a description |

We aim to acknowledge reports within 5 business days. Not every report results in removal — we assess each case against these Guidelines and applicable law.

---

## 10. Consequences

Depending on severity and history, we may:

1. **Warn** the account holder
2. **Remove or disable** specific content
3. **Pause uploads** (for example, pending AI disclosure review or elevated bot-risk score)
4. **Suspend** account access temporarily
5. **Terminate** the account permanently

Repeat infringers — including repeat copyright violators — will be terminated in accordance with our [Copyright Policy](/?page=copyright).

---

## 11. Appeals

If you believe we made an error, email [legal@indiefund.com](mailto:legal@indiefund.com) with your username, the action taken, and why you believe it was incorrect. We will review and respond within a reasonable time.

---

## 12. Updates

We may update these Guidelines as the Platform evolves. The current version is always available at `/?page=community-guidelines`.
