# IndieFund platform-specific privacy disclosures

These disclosures supplement the Privacy Policy above. They describe how IndieFund handles data beyond standard website practices.

> *Draft for review only — not legal advice. Requires review by an Australian technology lawyer before launch.*

---

## Who this policy applies to

IndieFund serves three account types:

- **Fans** — discover artists, subscribe, purchase, and engage
- **Artists** — upload content, sell products, receive support, run promotions
- **Hosts** — list venues and manage bookings through IndieFund | Spaces

If you opt in to share your email with an artist, that artist may contact you under their own practices.

---

## Platform-specific data we collect

### User-generated content

Artists and hosts upload music, artwork, posts, live stream metadata, store listings, and venue photos. Music uploads include AI disclosure level and subscriber-only settings.

### First-party journey analytics

We collect **server-side** usage events (not third-party ad or analytics cookies):

- Page views, artist profile visits, discovery interactions
- Music previews and full plays
- Follows, subscriptions, tips, and purchases
- Comment and like activity
- Email opt-in events
- Promotion campaign engagement (Discovery Ads)

If we add third-party analytics cookies in future, they will load **only after consent** via Iubenda. **Advertising and remarketing cookies will never be used.**

### Email sharing between fans and artists

Fans may opt in to share their email with supported artists. We record global consent, per-artist sharing status, and revocation timestamps. Artists may export mailing lists **only for fans who opted in**.

### Beta feedback

Beta testers may submit feedback including page path, summary, and browser user agent.

---

## Discovery Ads (first-party promotions)

Artists may run promoted releases with capped budgets ($5–$100). Billing is based on qualified fan engagement, not ad network impressions. This is **not** third-party advertising and does not use advertising cookies.

---

## Payments

All payments are processed by **Stripe**. We store transaction metadata and Stripe customer/subscription IDs but not full card numbers.

---

## We do not sell personal information

IndieFund does not sell personal data and **does not and will not** use advertising cookies or remarketing pixels.
