## 1. Eligibility

To use IndieFund as an Artist, you must:

- Be at least 16 years old
- Be an independent creator (or authorised representative of a creator or band)
- Provide accurate profile information including stage name, genre, city, and creative professions
- Complete your profile in good faith (avatar, story, point of view, and social links improve trust and discovery ranking)

IndieFund is for **independent human artists**. Fully AI-generated music is prohibited. AI-assisted or AI-collaborative work requires disclosure at upload.

## 3. Revenue and platform fees

### 3.1 Fee schedule

When fans support you through IndieFund, the following Platform Fees apply:

| Revenue stream | Platform fee | You receive |
|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| Monthly support and subscriptions | 10% | 90% |
| One-time tips | 10% | 90% |
| Music and merch sales | 15% | 85% |
| Custom commissions | 15% | 85% |

Fees are calculated on the gross amount paid by the fan before Stripe processing costs unless otherwise stated. The in-app fee schedule is the authoritative reference.

### 3.2 Live events

Ticket and door revenue splits are agreed between you and the venue. IndieFund does not take a cut of food and beverage revenue at hosted venues.

### 3.3 External fulfilment

You may link to external stores (Shopify, Bandcamp, Printful, Gelato, and similar). Sales completed off-platform are not subject to IndieFund's marketplace fee, but you are solely responsible for fulfilment, customer service, and compliance on those channels.

## 4. Payouts

### 4.1 Stripe Connect

To receive payouts, you must complete **Stripe Connect Express** onboarding and maintain an account in good standing. IndieFund uses Stripe to route your share of fan payments to your connected account.

### 4.2 Timing

Payout timing follows Stripe's standard schedule for your country and account status. IndieFund does not hold funds beyond what is necessary for transaction processing, dispute windows, and fraud review.

### 4.3 Failed or reversed payments

If a payment is refunded, charged back, or found fraudulent, the corresponding artist share may be reversed or offset against future payouts.

### 4.4 Taxes

You are solely responsible for income tax, GST, and other taxes on your earnings. IndieFund may request tax information as required by law.

## 7. Discovery Ads

You may run promotion campaigns for releases with:

- Budget between **$5 and $100** per campaign
- Maximum **4 active campaigns** at a time
- Billing based on **qualified fan engagement** (full listens, saves, follows, shares, purchases) — not raw impressions

Unused campaign budget may be refunded to your promotion wallet when you cancel a campaign.

## 8. Fan email and mailing lists

You may receive fan email addresses **only when fans opt in** to share their email with supported artists. You must:

- Use emails only for updates about your releases, shows, and exclusive content
- Honour unsubscribe and revocation requests
- Not sell or share fan emails with third parties

Exporting mailing lists is available on Artist Pro and Studio plans.

## 9. Trust, upload limits, and enforcement

IndieFund operates trust systems to protect the community:

- New or incomplete profiles may be limited to **3 uploads** until the profile is complete
- Elevated bot-risk scores may **pause uploads**
- **Two or more open AI review flags** pause uploads pending review
- Rapid upload velocity or suspicious patterns trigger review

You may not circumvent these systems. See the [Community Guidelines](/?page=community-guidelines).
