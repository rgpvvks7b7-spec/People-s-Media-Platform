## 1. Scope

This policy covers copyright and related intellectual property complaints regarding content hosted on IndieFund, including:

- Music uploads and streams
- Artwork and images
- Posts, Instants, and stories
- Live stream recordings
- Store product images and descriptions

For non-copyright issues (harassment, spam, AI policy violations), see our [Community Guidelines](/?page=community-guidelines).

**Note:** The in-app Instant **Report** button is for community guideline violations. It is not a substitute for a formal copyright notice under this policy.

## 2. Reporting copyright infringement

If you believe content on IndieFund infringes your copyright, send a notice to [copyright@indiefund.com](mailto:copyright@indiefund.com) with the following information:

### Required information

1. **Your identity** — Full legal name, email address, and (if applicable) company name and relationship to the rights holder
2. **Identification of the copyrighted work** — Describe the work or provide a URL to the original
3. **Identification of the infringing material** — Provide the specific IndieFund URL(s) or sufficient detail to locate the content (artist username, track title, post link)
4. **Good-faith statement** — A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
5. **Accuracy statement** — A statement, under penalty of perjury where applicable, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on the owner's behalf
6. **Signature** — Physical or electronic signature of the rights holder or authorised agent

### Example

> I, [Name], am the copyright owner of [work]. The following content infringes my rights: [URL]. I have a good-faith belief this use is unauthorised. The information in this notice is accurate. Signed, [Name].

## 3. Our response process

| Step | Timeline (business days) | Action |
|------|--------------------------|--------|
| Acknowledgement | Within 5 | We confirm receipt of your notice |
| Review | Within 10–15 | We assess completeness and merit |
| Action | As soon as practicable | Remove or disable access to disputed content if the notice is valid |
| Notification | Promptly | We notify the uploader of the takedown and reason |

We may contact you for additional information. Incomplete notices may delay processing.

## 4. Counter-notification

If your content was removed and you believe the takedown was mistaken or that you have the right to use the material, you may submit a counter-notification to [copyright@indiefund.com](mailto:copyright@indiefund.com).

### Required information

1. Your name, email, and IndieFund username
2. Identification of the removed content and its location before removal
3. A statement under penalty of perjury where applicable that you have a good-faith belief the content was removed due to mistake or misidentification
4. A statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the courts of Victoria, Australia (or your jurisdiction as advised by legal counsel)
5. Your physical or electronic signature

If we receive a valid counter-notification, we may restore the content after 10–14 business days unless the original complainant notifies us that they have filed a court action.

**Legal note:** Australian copyright law does not mirror the US DMCA exactly. An Australian technology lawyer should review this process before launch, especially if you serve US users and seek safe-harbour protections.

## 5. Repeat infringer policy

IndieFund maintains a policy of terminating accounts of repeat infringers.

| Strike | Typical action |
|--------|----------------|
| First valid complaint | Content removed; warning issued |
| Second valid complaint | Upload privileges suspended for 30 days |
| Third valid complaint | Account terminated |

We may escalate or skip steps for egregious violations (for example, commercial piracy or repeat uploads of the same infringing work).

Strike records are associated with the account and may consider linked accounts where we have evidence of evasion.

## 6. False or bad-faith claims

Submitting a false or materially misleading copyright notice or counter-notification may result in:

- Rejection of the claim
- Liability for damages under applicable law
- Account termination

Do not use copyright notices to harass artists or suppress lawful criticism.

## 7. User responsibilities

**Artists and uploaders** must ensure they have rights to all content they publish. This includes:

- Master recordings and compositions
- Samples and beats (licensed or cleared)
- Cover art and merchandise designs
- Video and promotional materials

See the [Artist Agreement](/?page=artist-agreement) for warranty and indemnity obligations.

## 8. Trademarks and other IP

This policy focuses on copyright. For trademark disputes (for example, unauthorised use of a brand name or logo), contact [copyright@indiefund.com](mailto:copyright@indiefund.com) with the same level of detail. A standalone Trademark Policy may be added in a future update.
