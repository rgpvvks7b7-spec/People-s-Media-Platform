## 1. Your IndieFund account

A Fan account lets you discover artists, save music, subscribe for exclusive content, purchase products, tip artists, attend live streams, buy event tickets, and participate in community features.

You must be at least 16 years old and provide accurate registration information including favourite genres and city or region (used for discovery and local gig matching).

---

## 2. Supporting artists

### 2.1 Subscriptions

Artists set support tiers starting from **$1 per month** (or equivalent). When you subscribe:

- You authorise recurring charges through **Stripe** until you cancel
- You receive access to subscriber-only content for the subscribed artist and profession
- You may manage or cancel subscriptions through the **Stripe Billing Portal**

Cancellation stops future charges. It does not automatically refund prior billing periods unless required by law.

### 2.2 Tips

One-time tips start from **$1**. Tips are voluntary and generally non-refundable once processed.

You may apply **discovery credits** earned through promoted content engagement toward tips, subject to per-transaction redemption caps displayed in the Platform.

### 2.3 What you receive

Benefits depend on what each artist offers — exclusive tracks, posts, live streams, discounts, or other perks. IndieFund does not guarantee specific content from any artist. Disputes about artist deliverables should first be raised with the artist.

---

## 3. Purchases

### 3.1 Store and digital downloads

You may purchase digital downloads, merch, beats, stems, vinyl, cassettes, and other products listed on artist stores. Digital purchases are typically delivered immediately upon payment confirmation.

### 3.2 Event tickets

You may purchase tickets for shows listed on IndieFund | Spaces. Unless stated otherwise, **one ticket per fan per show** applies. The artist and venue are responsible for the event itself; IndieFund facilitates ticketing.

### 3.3 External products

Some products link to external fulfilment (Shopify, Bandcamp, and similar). Purchases on external sites are governed by those sellers' terms.

### 3.4 Refunds

Except where required by the **Australian Consumer Law** or other applicable law:

- Digital goods and completed downloads are generally **non-refundable**
- Tips are **non-refundable**
- Physical goods and tickets follow the artist's or host's stated policy

Contact the artist or host first. If unresolved, contact [legal@indiefund.com](mailto:legal@indiefund.com).

---

## 4. Email sharing consent

You may opt in to share your email address with artists you support. This allows artists to send updates about releases, shows, and exclusive content.

- Opt in at registration, during support checkout, or in Profile settings
- Revoke anytime in Profile → email preferences
- Revoking global consent revokes sharing with **all** artists

See our [Privacy Policy](/?page=privacy) for details.

---

## 5. Discovery and promotions

IndieFund personalises artist recommendations based on your genres, location, saves, and discovery preferences. You may adjust settings including:

- Prefer emerging artists
- Fewer promoted items in discovery
- Promoted content only in your genres

**Discovery Ads** are paid promotions by artists. Engagement with promoted content may earn you small discovery credits. Promotion budgets are capped and billing is engagement-based — see the [Artist Agreement](/?page=artist-agreement).

---

## 6. Community participation

### 6.1 Comments and likes

You may comment on and like posts where the artist's comment settings allow. Follow the [Community Guidelines](/?page=community-guidelines).

### 6.2 Live chat

When attending live streams, chat respectfully. Artists may restrict chat to supporters.

### 6.3 Instants

You may view and report Instants using the in-app **Report** button. Reports should be good-faith violations of Community Guidelines, not copyright disputes (see [Copyright Policy](/?page=copyright)).

## 7. Your conduct

You must not:

- Harass artists or other fans
- Share subscriber-only content outside authorised access
- Circumvent payment or access controls
- Use automated tools to scrape or manipulate the Platform
- Submit false reports or fraudulent chargebacks

Violations may result in account suspension or termination.

All fan payments are processed by Stripe. IndieFund is not a party to direct agreements between you and artists except as described in the Terms.

You authorise Stripe to charge your selected payment method for subscriptions, tips, and purchases you initiate.

IndieFund may suspend or terminate your account for violations of this Agreement or the Community Guidelines.
