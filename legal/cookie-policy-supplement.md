# IndieFund platform-specific cookie disclosures

These disclosures supplement the Cookie Policy above.

> *Draft for review only — not legal advice. Requires review by an Australian technology lawyer before launch.*

---

## Our cookie philosophy

IndieFund **does not and will not** use advertising cookies, remarketing pixels, or third-party ad network tracking (Meta Pixel, Google Ads, TikTok Pixel, etc.).

If we add third-party **analytics** cookies in future (for example Google Analytics or Plausible), they will be loaded **only after you consent** through our Iubenda cookie banner. The **marketing/advertising category will never be enabled.**

---

## Strictly necessary cookies

| Name / type | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| **Session cookie** | Keeps you logged in | Session or up to 2 weeks |
| **CSRF token** (`csrftoken`) | Protects against cross-site request forgery | 1 year |

Configured with `SameSite=Lax` and `Secure` in production.

---

## Functional storage (local storage)

We use browser **local storage** for:

| Area | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Store cart | Shopping cart between visits |
| Player preferences | Volume and playback settings |
| Discovery mode | Discovery view preferences |
| UI state | Dismissed notices and interface preferences |
| Referral source | Artist page visit attribution |

---

## First-party analytics (no cookie)

Usage events (plays, purchases, page views) are recorded **on our servers**, not via third-party analytics cookies. See our [Privacy Policy](/?page=privacy).

---

## Discovery Ads

Artist promotion campaigns are **first-party** features billed on fan engagement. They are **not** third-party ad network cookies.

---

## Push notifications

Optional web push requires explicit browser permission. Managed by our service worker, not advertising trackers.

---

## Third-party cookies you may encounter

- **Stripe** checkout may set cookies during payment
- **Social embeds** (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) may set cookies if you interact with embedded content

We do not control third-party cookies from those providers.
