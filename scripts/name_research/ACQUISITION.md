### Trademark / brand conflict risk — HIGH

Even if you acquire the domain, existing **Catalynk** brands create confusion risk:

| Entity | Domain | Business |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Catalynk Limited (NZ) | catalynk.co | Knowledge-sharing / consulting since 2003 |
| Catalynk (UK startup) | catalynk.app | AI-powered clinical triage for GP clinics |
| Catalynk Limited (NZ, 2018) | — | Active company CIN 9429036019045 |

No exact "Catalynk" trademark found in IP Australia quick search, but **common-law and passing-off risk** exists in AU/NZ/UK due to active businesses using the name in different sectors.

### Recommendation

**Do not acquire unless:**
- Lawyer clears trademark in Classes 9, 41, 42 for AU + target markets
- You budget $3,000–$10,000 for domain alone
- You accept rebrand confusion with existing Catalynk entities

**Better alternative:** Register **ownlynk.com** or **launchlynk.com** (~$15) with cleaner TM landscape and same brand story.

| Name | Domain | Business |
|------|--------|----------|

## Immediate action if acquiring catalynk.com

1. Check [HugeDomains.com](https://www.hugedomains.com) for listing price
2. Run full trademark search: IP Australia, USPTO, UK IPO, NZ IPONZ
3. Assess confusion with catalynk.co (NZ consulting) and catalynk.app (UK healthcare AI)
4. If cleared, negotiate — start offer at 40–60% of asking price
5. Use escrow (Escrow.com or Dan.com) for transfer

## Immediate action if registering fresh (recommended)

1. Register **ownlynk.com** at Cloudflare/Namecheap/Porkbun (~$10–15)
2. Register **ownlynk.io** or **ownlynk.app** as backup ($12–30)
3. Claim @ownlynk on Instagram and X immediately
4. File AU trademark intent-to-use in Classes 9, 41, 42
